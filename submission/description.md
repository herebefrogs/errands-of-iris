Iris, the Greek goddess of rainbows and messenger of the Olympians, has  messages to deliver. Collect rainbow dust buried underground to grow her a rainbow bridge leading to her destination (because everybody knows rainbows don't just appear in the sky, but sprout from the ground instead, ain't that right?)

## Controls:

### Desktop
- Arrow keys/WASD and left-click mouse drag (virtual D-pad) to move
- Up/down to navigate menus, Enter/Space to select, Esc to go back

### Mobile
- Finger swipes (virtual D-pad) to move
- Tap menus item to select

## Gameplay:
- Collect rainbow dust (the more dust, the larger the rainbow, the higher your score)
- Game ends when you resurface or lose all momentum
- Sand (light brown) and clay (dark brown) slow you down, dense rainbow patches give you a speed boost
- Double-Rainbow easter egg
- Underground is infinite horizontally and vertically
- Terrain & dust distribution is seeded for deterministic runs. Select past seeds from highscore table or generate new ones from the menu
- Share seeds with friends so they can try beating your highscores.
